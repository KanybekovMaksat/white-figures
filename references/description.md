# Описание к рилсу

The reel ends with «Смотрите подробности в описании», so the description pays that promise. Russian, parents (mothers 30–45), «вы». The structure is the one Neuron Visuals uses under its 50–100M reels (studied 26.11.2026). Worked example: [`description-example.txt`](description-example.txt).

Order, top to bottom, each block separated by an empty line:

1. **Hook** = the moral in one line + 1–2 emoji.
2. **Recognition**: 3–4 tiny concrete scenes from the reader's own week, each a fragment with a real object in it («Шнурки, которые вы завязали сами, потому что опаздывали»). This block is what makes people send the reel.
3. **Turn**: one short line that flips the scenes.
4. **Fact**: one real finding with a number or a name, in plain words. Only a study you can name (author, year); if none is certain, write a plain observation without numbers.
5. **Action**: one thing for today or this week, starting with «Сегодня» / «На этой неделе», with a ready phrase in «кавычках» when it fits.
6. **Closing line**: 3–6 words, the reel's idea once more.
7. **Share line**: «Отправьте тому, кто …» naming one specific person.
8. **Hashtags**: exactly five, broad words people follow and search (#мотивация #дисциплина #воспитание #родители #саморазвитие #привычки #дети #психология #мышление #успех), chosen for the theme. Brand names stay out of hashtags.

The school is mentioned in at most every third description, as one line between 6 and 7 («В Codify дети 7–14 лет … Напишите «ПРОБА» в комментариях, пришлём запись на пробный урок.»). The others carry only the share line.

Length 600–900 characters. Prices, dates and promises of results stay out.
