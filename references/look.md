# The look

## World
Deep navy `#14213F` falling into black, a glossy black-glass floor with a soft reflection, one soft key spot per side, blue rim light, gentle bloom, film grain and vignette. Premium stylised 3D, never flat clip-art. All of it is already in every bundled scene: keep those sections as they are.

## Figures
Faceless, genderless, white `#F4F6FB` satin bodies: sphere head, capsule torso, thick rounded limbs. They stand for "a child" or "a parent" by size only (parent = child × 1.43). Body language carries everything: slumped shoulders, hugging knees, arms up. Motion is smooth and weighty, with squash on landings. Real children are never shown.

- Moral at y≈545 and 605: 800, 44 px (42 if needed to fit 1000 px), fades in at 8.0 s with a slight rise.
- Counter, if the story has one: top centre y≈400, 700, 40 px.
- End card from 13.6 s: soft dark band y 1380–1640; «СМОТРИТЕ ПОДРОБНОСТИ» at y 1478 and «В ОПИСАНИИ» at y 1522 (700, 34 px, tracking 0.14 em, white 90 %); a lime chevron at y 1566 bobbing ±6 px. Code: the `// end card` block in scenes 10–13.
- The frame carries no logo and no watermark.

## Frame
1080×1920, 30 fps, 450 frames. Instagram covers the top 230 px, the bottom 320 px and the right 120 px between y 900–1600: keep figures, text and props out of those.
